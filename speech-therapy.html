Speech Therapy

expand_more

Our speech-language pathology goal is maximizing a patient’s functional independence in communicating and swallowing as well as increasing the patient’s and caregiver’s knowledge of speech, language and swallowing disorders, and of appropriate treatment processes.

Speech-language Pathology Services include:

  • Language evaluation and training
  • Swallowing evaluation and training
  • Communication device training
  • Speech Disorders
  • Language Disorders
  • Feeding/Swallowing Disorders
  • Oral-motor Disorders
  • Cognitive Therapy associated with Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)
  • Voice
  • Fluency
  • Aural Rehabilitation – Hearing Loss, Cochlear Implants
  • Augmentative / Alternative Communication Devices
  • Play Therapy / Social Skills
  • Picky Eaters / Feeding Therapy
